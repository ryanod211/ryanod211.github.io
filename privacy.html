Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Video Studio is a private set of tools used by one person, Ryan O’Donnell, to edit and publish videos on his own YouTube channel and read its analytics. It has no other users.

What Google data it uses

With his permission, Video Studio uses the following Google API access for his own YouTube channel only:

  • YouTube Analytics (read-only), including revenue reports: views, watch time, retention, traffic and similar reports for his videos.
  • YouTube Data (read-only): the list of his videos, their details and the comments on them.
  • YouTube upload and management: to upload his finished videos, schedule them, and set their thumbnails, descriptions and captions.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to review how his videos perform and to publish them to his own channel.
  • It is stored on his own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in a private folder on that computer and are never shared.
  • Comment text is read without commenters’ names.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Data stored on his computer can be deleted by him at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Ryan O’Donnell YouTube channel.
